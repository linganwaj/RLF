Building a Healthier Rwanda Together

RLF prioritizes the health and well-being of Rwandans, women and children, the promotion of health literacy throughout the nation and implements impactful community development initiatives . It is a collective effort, empowering others to become agents of change. We are building a network of support, a community of hope, a movement that will flourish for generations.

About Us

Unleashing Potential with Creative Strategy

The Rucci Legacy Foundation (RFL) is a non-governmental, non-profit organization born from a Rwandan family’s long- standing commitment for their nation. With deep roots in the nation's public and private sectors and expertise in health, they’ve launched RLF in 2024 to foster healthier and more resilient communities across Rwanda.

  • Mission
    To cultivate sustainable legacies of compassion by empowering Rwandan communities through transformative healthcare initiatives, focused on women and children, and fostering widespread health literacy, thereby driving systemic change and ensuring equitable opportunity for all.

  • Vision
    To forge a future where compassionate action drives sustainable transformation in Rwanda, cultivating healthy, informed, and empowered communities that lead their own development and build a legacy of well-being for generations to come.
